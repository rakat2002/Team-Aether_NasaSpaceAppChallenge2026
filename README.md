# 🚀 Astrorelics: Space Kids (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026)

> **"NASA's missions may end, but their stories don't."**[cite: 2]  
> **"Abandoned... but NEVER forgotten!"**[cite: 1, 2]  
> An interactive learning platform turning robotic explorers, orbiters, landers, and lunar relics left across our solar system into gamified, educational adventures for young learners[cite: 1, 2].

---

## 🌌 Overview & The Challenge

Across NASA's history, robotic explorers and spacecraft have traveled outward into deep space, the Moon, and Mars—often without a return journey[cite: 2]. When missions end, their hardware remains behind as historic time capsules[cite: 2]. Without context, students only see inanimate machines in photographs[cite: 2]. 

**Astrorelics** bridges this gap by transforming NASA spacecraft, rovers, and scientific instruments into interactive, story-driven missions[cite: 2]. Students uncover why each machine was sent, how it operated, the engineering hurdles it overcame, the discoveries it made, and the legacy it left behind[cite: 2].

Built for the **NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026** under the challenge theme: **"Abandoned but not Forgotten."**[cite: 1]

---

## ✨ Features

- 🪐 **Interactive Live Solar System Explorer:** Track historical robotic relics across interplanetary trajectories and planetary orbits (including Opportunity, Spirit, Voyager 1 & 2, Apollo 11 Lunar Module Descent Stage, Viking 1, Curiosity, and Perseverance & Ingenuity)[cite: 1, 2].
- 📖 **Story-Driven Chapter Journeys:** Walk through multi-chapter operational chronologies (launch, deep-space transit, high-stakes descent, multi-year survival, and mission milestones like the 42.195 km Martian Marathon)[cite: 1, 2].
- 🕹️ **Interactive Engineering & Science Minigames:**
  - *Descent Sequence Control:* Step through parachute deployment, retrorockets, airbags, and touchdown sequences[cite: 1, 2].
  - *Martian Sand Canvas:* Navigate rovers across planetary terrain and examine tire-track Morse code patterns[cite: 1, 2].
  - *Spectrometry Detective:* Interpret Mössbauer spectrometer data to verify signs of ancient Martian liquid water[cite: 1, 2].
  - *Dust Storm & Solar Energy Tracker:* Monitor solar array wattage dropoffs during severe Martian dust storms[cite: 1, 2].
  - *Deep Space Network (DSN) Transceiver:* Beam wake-up signals across millions of kilometers of interplanetary space[cite: 1, 2].
- 🗂️ **Mission Summary Flashcards:** Flip interactive telemetry flashcards showing hardware specs, launch dates, distances traveled, and historical significance[cite: 1, 2].
- 🧠 **Cosmic Detective Challenges & Quizzes:** Brainstorming questions and interactive multi-choice quizzes that test and reinforce STEM concepts after every chapter[cite: 1, 2].
- 🏅 **NASA Space Badge Collection:** Earn XP, unlock digital mission patches (such as *The Little Marathoner* and *Galactic Master Artist*), and level up astronaut ranks[cite: 1, 2].
- 🎨 **Space Coloring Studio:** Sketch, highlight, and color NASA spacecraft blueprints using cosmic palettes, neon glow tools, and stamps, with export options to share artwork[cite: 1, 2].
- 🛠️ **Spacecraft Workshop:** Design, configure, and customize space probes from scratch by selecting chassis, mobility systems, power sources, sensor masts, and science payloads[cite: 1, 2].

## 🛰️ NASA Data & APIs Used

The platform utilizes authentic imagery, mission catalogs, and telemetry blueprints from official NASA sources:

| Resource / API | Endpoint / Reference | Application Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NASA Image and Video Library API** | `https://images-api.nasa.gov` | High-definition archival photos, rover camera feeds, and mission assets. |
| **Mars Rover Photos API** | `https://api.nasa.gov/mars-photos` | Real surface captures and mast telemetry from Opportunity, Spirit, and Curiosity. |
| **NASA 3D Resources** | `github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources` | Blueprint geometries and 3D spacecraft component references. |
| **NSSDCA Lunar Artifacts Catalog** | `nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar` | Landing coordinates and status records of lunar descent stages and instruments. |
| **Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD)** | `https://api.nasa.gov/planetary/apod` | Deep-space backgrounds and astronomical imagery. |
