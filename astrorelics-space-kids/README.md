# 🚀 ASTRORELICS: NASA Space Archaeology Academy for Kids

An engaging, interactive STEM learning experience combining **humankind's brave abandoned spacecraft**, **official-style cute NASA embroidered mission patches**, **story summary flashcards**, **photorealistic NASA imagery**, and an **improved multi-layer spacecraft coloring studio**.

Merged from **Option 1 (`nasa-space-kids`)** and **Option 2 (`astrorelics_space`)**.

---

## ✨ Features Added & Merged

### 1. 🎴 Cosmic Story Summary Flashcards (from Option 2)
- **3D Flip Flashcard Modal**: Smooth flip animation between front (high-resolution photo & mission stats) and back (Story Summary & Discovery).
- **Three-Stage Narrative Summaries**:
  - **The Birth**: Why engineers built the spacecraft.
  - **The Mission**: Historic explorations, distances traveled, and photos taken.
  - **Twilight & Eternal Legacy**: Why it was respectfully left behind across the Solar System.
- **Permanent Cosmic Flashcards Archive**: Browse and review all 8 key spacecraft:
  - Opportunity Rover (Mars)
  - Apollo 11 Lunar Module "Eagle" (Moon)
  - Voyager 1 & Voyager 2 (Interstellar Space & Grand Tour)
  - Viking 1 Lander (Mars Chryse Planitia)
  - Cassini Orbiter & Huygens (Saturn & Titan)
  - Spirit Rover (Gusev Crater)
  - Perseverance Rover & Ingenuity Helicopter (Jezero Crater)
  - Curiosity Rover (Mount Sharp)

### 2. 🎖️ Cute NASA-Style Embroidered Mission Badges (Inspiring Names & Appreciation)
- Modeled after authentic NASA embroidered mission patches (inspired by A-B Emblem, NASA's official patch manufacturer):
  - Distinct patch shapes: **Shield**, **Circle**, **Chevron**, **Hexagon**, and **Oval**.
  - Gold & silver thread borders, NASA vector arcs, constellation stars, and ribbon banners.
- Inspiring cute names appreciating the explorer:
  - 🏃‍♂️ **The Little Marathoner** (Opportunity Rover — 45km Mars Odyssey)
  - 🌙 **First Moonwalker Friend** (Apollo 11 — Tranquility Base)
  - 🛰️ **Interstellar Dreamer** (Voyager 1 — Golden Record to the Stars)
  - 🪐 **Lord of the Rings** (Cassini-Huygens — Saturn Rings & Titan)
  - 🔴 **Red Planet Pioneer** (Viking 1 — First Eyes on Martian Soil)
  - 🦾 **Brave Spirit Warrior** (Spirit — Turning Obstacles into Discoveries)
  - 🚁 **Sky Hopper & Seeker** (Perseverance & Ingenuity — Alien Flight Wings)
  - 🔬 **Curious Mountain Detective** (Curiosity — Mount Sharp Laser Explorer)
  - 🧠 **Master Cosmic Sleuth** (Cosmic Quiz 100% Champion)
  - 🔍 **Star Cadet Inquirer** (Cosmic Quiz Discovery)
  - 🎨 **Galactic Master Artist** (Coloring Studio Masterpiece)
  - 🛠️ **Starship Architect** (Build Workshop Engineer)
  - ☀️ **Solar Ray Master** (Clean Solar Energy Scientist)
- **Badge Celebration Modal**: Animated confetti bursts, sound effects, and encouraging appreciation commendations when completing stories and quizzes!
- **Persistent Flight Jacket Patch Board**: Accessible from the header, tracking total patches earned, XP, and badge lore.

### 3. 🎨 Improved Spacecraft Coloring Studio (Freehand Scratch & Drag)
- As requested, kept the **scratch & drag / brush coloring** mechanism from Option 1 (not the click-to-fill of Option 2).
- **Multi-layer Canvas System**:
  - The crisp spacecraft line-art outline is drawn on a protected top layer, meaning kids can scratch and paint freely without erasing or smudging the spacecraft outlines!
- **Good Spacecraft Selection**:
  - Opportunity Rover on Mars (with solar wings, camera eyes, and dust devil)
  - Apollo 11 Lunar Module (with gold foil patterns, descent engine, ladder, and Earth)
  - Voyager 1 (with 3.7m dish, Golden Record, and magnetometer boom)
  - Viking 1 Lander (with soil trench scoop arm and tripod footpads)
  - Cassini Orbiter (with Huygens probe and Saturn's sweeping rings)
  - Perseverance & Ingenuity (with robotic turret arm and flying helicopter drone)
- **Brush Tools**:
  - Smooth Pen
  - Neon Glow Brush
  - Star Stamp (scatters tiny twinkling stars while scratching)
  - Clean Eraser
  - Brush Size Slider (4px to 36px)
  - 12 Cosmic Palette colors + Custom Color Picker
  - Undo, Clear Canvas, Download PNG artwork, and "Finish Artwork" badge unlock!

### 4. 🧠 Cosmic Detective Quiz & Activities
- Questions merged from Option 1 & Option 2 (Opportunity, Viking 1, Cassini planetary protection, Apollo 11 plaque, Voyager 1).
- Interactive 3D Solar System explorer with orbit simulation.
- Build-a-Spaceship workshop and Photovoltaic solar power science explainer.
